## Literal mode: explicitly regularized extension, not an exact theorem test

Let U be the FULL continuous Fourier transform of RR Keys cubic a=-1/2,
including its lobes/zeros beyond Nyquist, and H_S(w)=product[j>=0] U(w/S^j).
Numerically use18 factors. At elementary reduction r, let A_r(w)=U(rw).
The unrestricted ratio H_S(rw)/H_S(w) can have poles. We therefore use the
following anchor-preserving Tikhonov realization, with eta=0.001 fixed BEFORE
any literal-mode SR results:

    K_r = A_r + H_S * (H_S(rw) - A_r*H_S) / (H_S^2 + eta^2).

It minimizes |H_S*K-H_S(rw)|^2 + eta^2*|K-A_r|^2 per frequency. The ridge is
centered on the literal AA kernel, rather than zero, to preserve the shared
anchor exactly: H_S(Sw)=U(Sw)*H_S(w), hence K_S=U(Sw) for every eta. This is a
documented extension of the paper's Tikhonov construction, not a claim that
literal aliased bicubic meets its alias-free assumptions. No nonnegative
physical PSF or exact sampled composition is asserted for this branch.

Implementation evaluates A_r analytically in SPACE (the literal scaled cubic)
and adds an inverse-Fourier correction. Thus the base is never Nyquist-cut;
the transform of the correction is integrated to32*pi, with FFT length2^20
and spatial spacing1/512. Pre-score convergence tests double the Fourier
period, extent, and spatial support. eta was not chosen from scores/images.
The first image is evaluated only after these numerical gates pass.

Synthetic spectral diagnostics before training: maximum ridge perturbation
of the exact quotient on |w|<=pi is <2e-6 across all requested r; tails of the
spatial correction beyond radius32*S have L1<1e-6. These bounds describe
numerical behavior, not performance or exact consistency under aliasing.

At m=1 the implementation calls literal RR cubic AA directly and reuses that
single network for ours/AA. All fractional kernels use the same fixed rule.
Results must explicitly identify literal-mode ours as a regularized extension;
do not silently present it as the exact band-limited operator.
